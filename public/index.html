Rules for big transactions

1
Spend right after statement date: Gives maximum 40–50 days of interest-free credit and pushes billing after your 1st of the month salary.
2
Avoid large purchases 1–2 days before statement close: Billed immediately in the next 15–20 days, causing immediate month-end cash crunch.
3
Keep utilization under 30%: If a card's current cycle spend nears 30% of its limit, switch to your runner-up card or UPI buffer.
4
Use UPI on statement days: Instant settlement ensures pending transactions don't accidentally close into the same day's statement.
